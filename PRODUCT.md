# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students tracking attendance across courses and planning safe absences.

## Product Purpose

Track course attendance and help students understand how many classes they can miss while remaining at their target attendance level.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The interface includes per-course attendance tracking, a daily timetable, attendance marking, target attendance, a bunk planner, and local storage persistence. Preserve all existing attendance calculations, timetable logic, bunk-planner math, and local storage behavior during UI changes.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing Attendance Tracker name and supplied app/payment assets.

## Product Principles

- Attendance metrics must remain accurate and understandable.
- Attendance marking should be direct and dependable.
- Daily schedules should be easy to scan.
- Student data should continue to persist locally.
